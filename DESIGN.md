# Design direction: seeshaun.xyz

- Identity: personal creator portfolio for seeshaun, focused on video editing, motion graphics, and experiments.
- Personality: expressive, precise, internet-native, slightly abrasive in its energy, but composed enough to feel like a real working creator's portfolio.
- Palette: near-black graphite, warm off-white type, electric acid-lime accent used sparingly for active states and key interactions.
- Typography: strong condensed display face for editorial statements, neutral sans-serif for readable interface and metadata.
- Mood: experimental, dark, image-led, asymmetric editorial composition. Motion should support previews and transitions rather than decorate every element.
- Layout: generous margins, sharp dividers, numbered sections, varied media scale, compact metadata, and a responsive single-column fallback.
- Avoid: generic gradients, repeated glass cards, fake metrics, placeholder claims, excessive rounded corners, glow on every element, and template-like hero sections.

- Design Read: personal portfolio for creators and potential collaborators, in a dark editorial visual language with experimental motion, dial ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.
- ENERGY 3: oversized typographic statement, vivid lime used as a single signal, and direct copy give the page a strong entrance.
- RHYTHM 3: a split opening, staggered video grid, simple project index, and portrait-led about section create varied section silhouettes.
- MOTION 2: video hover previews and short transitions clarify interactivity; reduced-motion preferences are respected. No scroll choreography or parallax is used.
- Identity motif: slash-mark wordmark, archive-style media numbering, thin dividers, and acid-lime section markers connect the portfolio to an editing timeline without turning the page into a software dashboard.
